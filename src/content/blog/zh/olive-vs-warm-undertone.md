## "你一定是暖调"——给橄榄肤的错误建议

Veins 偏绿、易晒黑、暖色有时 work——但 olive 不是 warm。Olive 是 modifier，不是 base undertone。

---

## 为何 olive 既非 warm 也非 cool

**Olive 是 modifier：** 可在 warm/cool/balanced base 之上——warm olive、cool olive、neutral olive

**Practically：** 暖色需 **muted** 而非 golden；冷色需 **soft** 而非 icy

---

## 误导的测试结果

**Vein test：** Olive veins 是 greenish-blue/teal，非因 golden base

**Tanning：** Olive 晒成 golden-brown（非 red），cool olive 也易晒黑

**Jewelry：** Gold "okay" 非 clearly flattering = olive indicator

**Warm colors：** Muted warm work 但 bright orange 不 = 你是 olive 非 warm

---

## 对比表要点

| | Warm | Olive |
|---|---|---|
| Cast | Golden/yellow | Green-grey |
| Warm foundation | 融合 | Orange |
| Bright orange | 常 flattering | Sallow |

---

## 色彩匹配差异

**Foundation：** Warm 融合 seamlessly；Olive 需 yellow-green shades

**Lip：** Warm=coral/peach；Olive=berry/mauve/brick

**Hair：** Warm=golden/copper；Olive=chocolate/muted auburn

**Clothing：** Warm=clear warm；Olive=muted earthy

---

## 核心要点

- Olive 是 modifier，非 warm undertone
- Bright vs muted test 是 decisive
- Warm olive 存在但需 muted earthy tones
- Foundation orange = 非 standard warm
