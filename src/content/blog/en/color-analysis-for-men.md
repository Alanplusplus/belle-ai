## Why Color Analysis Matters for Men

Most men's style advice focuses on fit and occasion, ignoring the enormous impact color has on how you look. The right shirt color can make you look healthier, more authoritative, and more attractive — without changing anything else about the outfit.

Color analysis answers the questions men actually face:
- Should I wear a navy or charcoal suit?
- Does this blue shirt look better than the white one?
- Why do I look washed out in some ties and great in others?
- Should my watch be silver or gold?

## How Color Analysis Works for Men

The same principles apply regardless of gender. Your seasonal color type is determined by:

1. **Undertone** — Whether your skin leans warm (golden/yellow) or cool (pink/blue)
2. **Depth** — How light or dark your overall coloring is
3. **Contrast** — The difference between your hair, skin, and eye color
4. **Chroma** — Whether your coloring is vivid/clear or muted/soft

## Men's Season Types

### Spring Men

**You might be a Spring if:**
- Golden, peachy, or warm beige skin
- Hair: Golden blonde, strawberry blonde, warm brown, or ginger
- Eyes: Green, hazel, warm brown, or blue with golden flecks
- You look great in: Salmon shirts, tan suits, warm green

**Wardrobe recommendations:**
- **Suits:** Tan, camel, warm navy, khaki
- **Shirts:** Salmon, peach, warm white (cream/ivory), sage green
- **Ties:** Coral, orange, warm gold patterns, turquoise
- **Casual:** Earth tones with warm cast, warm denim, olive
- **Metals:** Gold watch, brass belt buckle

### Summer Men

**You might be a Summer if:**
- Pink, rosy, or neutral-cool skin
- Hair: Ash blonde, ash brown, mousy, or gray
- Eyes: Soft blue, gray, gray-green, cool brown
- You look great in: Light blue shirts, medium gray suits

**Wardrobe recommendations:**
- **Suits:** Medium gray, blue-gray, soft navy
- **Shirts:** Sky blue, lavender, soft white, dusty pink (yes, really)
- **Ties:** Burgundy, muted blue patterns, silver-gray
- **Casual:** Washed denim, gray tones, muted blues, sage
- **Metals:** Silver watch, platinum or white gold

### Autumn Men

**You might be an Autumn if:**
- Golden, olive, warm tan, or bronze skin
- Hair: Dark brown with warm cast, auburn, chestnut, dark ginger
- Eyes: Dark brown, hazel, warm green, amber
- You look great in: Olive, rust, chocolate brown

**Wardrobe recommendations:**
- **Suits:** Chocolate brown, olive, warm charcoal, tan
- **Shirts:** Cream, warm blue, terracotta, olive, warm white
- **Ties:** Burgundy, forest green, rust patterns, gold
- **Casual:** Olive green, burnt orange, warm denim, leather
- **Metals:** Gold watch, copper/bronze accents

### Winter Men

**You might be a Winter if:**
- Cool or neutral-cool skin (olive to dark, or very fair with cool cast)
- Hair: Dark brown to black, or salt-and-pepper with cool tone
- Eyes: Dark brown, black, bright blue, or gray contrasting with dark features
- You look great in: Black suits, pure white shirts, bold ties

**Wardrobe recommendations:**
- **Suits:** Black, charcoal, navy, dark gray
- **Shirts:** Pure white, icy blue, light gray, bold blue
- **Ties:** True red, royal blue, black, sharp contrast patterns
- **Casual:** Black, white, bold contrast combinations, cool-toned denim
- **Metals:** Silver, platinum, stainless steel watch

## Practical Applications

### Job Interviews

Your season determines your "power combination":
- **Spring:** Tan suit + coral tie → Approachable yet confident
- **Summer:** Gray suit + burgundy tie → Professional and calm
- **Autumn:** Charcoal-brown suit + forest green tie → Grounded authority
- **Winter:** Charcoal suit + true red tie → Maximum command presence

### Casual Wear

Even in a t-shirt and jeans:
- **Spring:** Warm-toned t-shirt colors (sage, peach, warm blue) with tan/medium wash jeans
- **Summer:** Cool-muted t-shirt colors (slate, dusty blue, gray) with medium-cool wash jeans
- **Autumn:** Earthy t-shirt colors (olive, rust, forest) with warm wash or dark jeans
- **Winter:** Bold or neutral t-shirt colors (black, white, navy, true red) with dark wash jeans

### Grooming

Your season affects:
- **Glasses frames:** Warm tortoiseshell (Spring/Autumn) vs. black or silver (Summer/Winter)
- **Facial hair:** Consider whether your beard/stubble creates warm or cool shadows
- **Hair product:** Pomade with warm sheen (Spring/Autumn) vs. matte finish (Summer/Winter)
- **Skincare:** SPF and moisturizer — cool undertones tend to burn more, warm undertones tan

## Common Men's Color Mistakes

### "Navy is universal"

Navy comes in warm navy (greenish cast → better for Autumn/Spring) and cool navy (purplish cast → better for Winter/Summer). The wrong navy can look off.

### "White shirts work for everyone"

Pure stark white is a Winter color. Spring and Autumn men look better in cream, ivory, or off-white. Summer men suit soft white with a barely-blue tint.

### "Gray is neutral"

Warm gray (with yellow/brown undertone) suits Spring/Autumn. Cool gray (with blue/purple undertone) suits Summer/Winter. Wearing the wrong gray temperature can make you look sickly.

### "Black is always safe"

Black is actually only ideal for Winter men. On Springs, it's overwhelming. On Summers, it's too harsh. On Autumns, it drains color from the face. If you're not a Winter, swap black for your darkest appropriate neutral (dark brown for Autumn, charcoal for Summer, deep warm gray for Spring).
