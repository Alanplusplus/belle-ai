## Quick Answer: Which AI Color Analysis Tool Should You Use?

There is no single best tool, because the tools do different jobs. Here is the short version:

- **Fastest free check:** a free web tool such as Palette Hunt, Color-Analysis.app or the free tool from MyColorAnalysis.
- **Wardrobe and shopping app:** Dressika, which adds capsule wardrobe and hair color try-on features.
- **A human to double-check the AI:** MyColorAnalysis offers an optional expert review, or book a professional draping session.
- **Color, makeup and face shape in one report:** Belle AI, which is the tool we build (see the disclosure below).

[Belle AI](/) reads your selfie and returns a 12-season result with a visual report: palette, colors to avoid, makeup and wardrobe guidance. The same account also covers face shape, hair color, glasses and other analysis types.

### Dressika

A mobile app (iOS and Android) built around wardrobe planning. You can run an automatic photo analysis or answer a manual quiz, then use your palette for outfits, makeup shades and hair color previews. Choose it if you want a phone app for shopping and capsule wardrobe building. It has a limited free tier and an optional premium plan, so check the current pricing in your store.

### Colorwise

Independent reviews describe it as a hands-on tool where you pick your own skin, hair and eye colors and judge the result against seasonal palettes, rather than getting an AI verdict. It also sits next to a styling app. Choose it if you like to learn the system by doing it yourself. Remember that a 4-season result is less specific than a 12-season one.

### MyColorAnalysis AI

A free web tool that returns a 12-season result without an account. It also sells an optional human-reviewed analysis (about $39 at the time of writing), which is the main reason to pick it: a person checks the AI answer. Makeup and face-shape guidance is lighter than in a full beauty report.

### Palette Hunt

A quick, free web check that is easy to try in a couple of minutes. It is best used as a first impression or a second opinion, not as your final answer.

### Color-Analysis.app

A free web app (installable as a PWA) with a quiz-first flow and an optional photo. It also has a large library of color education articles. It is a good no-cost option if you prefer not to upload a photo.

## How Accurate Are AI Color Analysis Tools?

None of these tools is a certified colorist. AI judges colors from a photo, so the result depends on lighting, your camera, white balance and makeup. We do not publish an accuracy figure for any tool, and we are not aware of an independent test that measures how often AI agrees with professional draping.

To get the most out of any tool:

1. Use soft daylight facing a window, with no direct sun on your face.
2. Remove makeup and pull hair away from your face.
3. Use a plain background and no filters.
4. Take two or three photos and check that the result is stable.
5. Run a second tool and compare. If both land in the same family, you are probably close.
6. Confirm a borderline result with a [simple draping test at home](/blog/how-to-do-color-analysis-at-home).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the best free AI color analysis?

It depends on what you need. Palette Hunt, Color-Analysis.app and the free web tool from MyColorAnalysis give a quick result at no cost. Belle AI gives 3 free previews with the details blurred, and Dressika has a limited free tier. For a quick first answer a free web tool is enough; for a full palette plus makeup and wardrobe guidance, expect to pay for at least one report.

### Are AI color analysis apps accurate?

They are a good starting point, not a verdict. Results change with lighting, camera and makeup, and no independent test has measured how often AI matches a professional draping. Take two or three photos, compare two tools, and confirm borderline results with a simple draping test or a professional.

### Do I need to upload a photo for a color analysis?

Not always. Quiz-based tools, including the free Belle AI color quiz, ask about your veins, jewelry and sun response instead. A photo-based AI analysis looks at your actual skin, hair and eye coloring, so it is usually more specific than a quiz.

### Should I use a 4-season or 12-season tool?

A 12-season tool is more useful. The 4-season system lumps very different palettes together, such as Warm Spring and Warm Autumn, while the 12-season system gives you sub-seasons and specific shades for makeup, clothing and hair.
