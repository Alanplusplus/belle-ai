## What Does "Color Season" Mean?

In personal color analysis, your "season" refers to the group of colors that harmonize best with your natural coloring. The system uses the four seasons as metaphors:

- **Spring** — Warm, light, clear colors (like flowers blooming)
- **Summer** — Cool, light, soft colors (like a hazy beach day)
- **Autumn** — Warm, deep, muted colors (like falling leaves)
- **Winter** — Cool, deep, clear colors (like a crisp snowy landscape)

Your season is determined by three factors: your undertone (warm or cool), your depth (light or deep), and your chroma (bright/clear or soft/muted).

## Quick Season Finder

Answer these three questions to get a fast estimate:

### Question 1: Am I Warm or Cool?

Look at the veins on your inner wrist in natural light:
- Green veins → **Warm** (Spring or Autumn)
- Blue/purple veins → **Cool** (Summer or Winter)
- Mix of both → Could be either; move to Question 2

### Question 2: Am I Light or Deep?

Consider your overall coloring (skin + hair + eyes combined):
- Light hair + light eyes + fair skin → **Light** (Spring or Summer)
- Dark hair + dark eyes + medium/dark skin → **Deep** (Autumn or Winter)
- Somewhere in between → Move to Question 3 for clarity

### Question 3: Am I Bright or Soft?

Look at the contrast between your features:
- High contrast (dark brows on light skin, vivid eyes) → **Bright/Clear**
- Low contrast (everything blends together softly) → **Soft/Muted**

### Combining Your Answers

| Undertone | Depth | = Season |
|-----------|-------|----------|
| Warm | Light | **Spring** |
| Cool | Light | **Summer** |
| Warm | Deep | **Autumn** |
| Cool | Deep | **Winter** |

## Detailed Season Profiles

### Spring

**You might be a Spring if:**
- Your skin has a warm, golden, or peachy glow
- You tan easily and rarely burn
- Your natural hair has warm highlights (golden, strawberry, copper)
- Your eyes are warm (green, hazel, warm brown, blue with golden ring)
- Wearing peach, coral, or warm green makes you look healthy
- Black clothing looks too harsh on you

**Spring Sub-Types:**

- **Light Spring:** Very fair with delicate warm coloring. Think blonde hair, light eyes, peachy skin. Best in pastels with warm undertones.
- **Warm Spring:** Obviously warm coloring. Golden blonde to auburn hair, warm skin. Best in rich warm colors without being too dark.
- **Bright Spring:** Warm with high clarity. Clear, vivid coloring with sparkle. Best in saturated warm colors.

**Celebrity examples:** Emma Stone, Amy Adams, Blake Lively, Jennifer Lawrence

### Summer

**You might be a Summer if:**
- Your skin has a cool pink or neutral undertone
- You burn before you tan, or tan to a pink/grayish tone
- Your natural hair is ash blonde, ash brown, or mousy
- Your eyes are cool (gray-blue, soft blue, gray-green, muted brown)
- Wearing lavender, dusty rose, or slate blue makes you look radiant
- Orange or golden colors make you look sallow

**Summer Sub-Types:**

- **Light Summer:** Very fair with soft cool coloring. Ash blonde hair, light eyes. Best in cool pastels.
- **Cool Summer:** Distinctly cool with medium depth. Rose or blue undertone. Best in clear cool colors.
- **Soft Summer:** Cool-neutral with very muted coloring. Low contrast. Best in dusty, grayed tones.

**Celebrity examples:** Cate Blanchett, Kate Middleton, Jennifer Aniston, Taylor Swift

### Autumn

**You might be an Autumn if:**
- Your skin has a warm, golden, olive, or amber undertone
- You tan to a golden or brown tone
- Your natural hair is warm brown, auburn, red, or dark with warm undertones
- Your eyes are warm (hazel, amber, warm brown, green)
- Wearing olive, rust, or mustard makes your skin glow
- Cool pastels (lavender, icy pink) wash you out

**Autumn Sub-Types:**

- **Soft Autumn:** Warm-neutral with very muted coloring. Blended, gentle warmth. Best in soft earthy tones.
- **Warm Autumn:** Obviously warm with medium-deep coloring. Rich golden quality. Best in spiced, warm colors.
- **Deep Autumn:** Very deep coloring with warm undertone. Dark hair, dark eyes. Best in rich, warm darks.

**Celebrity examples:** Jessica Alba, Drew Barrymore, Beyoncé, Julia Roberts

### Winter

**You might be a Winter if:**
- Your skin has a cool or neutral-cool undertone
- High contrast between your skin, hair, and eyes
- Your natural hair is dark brown to black
- Your eyes are dark or strikingly bright against your skin
- Wearing pure white, black, or jewel tones makes you look powerful
- Muted or earthy colors make you look "off"

**Winter Sub-Types:**

- **Deep Winter:** Very deep coloring, dark everything with cool undertone. Best in rich, dark cool colors.
- **Cool Winter:** Distinctly cool with medium-to-deep depth. Icy quality. Best in true cool colors from light to dark.
- **Bright Winter:** Cool with extremely high contrast and vivid coloring. Best in bold, saturated colors.

**Celebrity examples:** Lupita Nyong'o, Anne Hathaway, Lily Collins, Fan Bingbing

## What If I'm Between Two Seasons?

It's very common to fall between two seasons. This usually happens when:

- **Soft Summer vs. Soft Autumn** — You're neutral-toned (neither clearly warm nor cool) with muted coloring
- **Bright Spring vs. Bright Winter** — You're neutral-toned with vivid, clear coloring
- **Deep Autumn vs. Deep Winter** — You're neutral-toned with very deep coloring
- **Light Spring vs. Light Summer** — You're neutral-toned with very light coloring

If you're between seasons, you can often wear colors from both palettes. Focus on the shared characteristics (both Soft seasons share muted tones; both Bright seasons share vivid tones) and experiment with the borderline colors.
