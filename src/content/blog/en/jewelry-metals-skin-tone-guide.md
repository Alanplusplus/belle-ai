## The Metal Question

It's one of the most common style dilemmas: should you wear gold or silver? The answer isn't about preference or trends — it's about what makes your skin look its best.

The "right" metal for you is the one that harmonizes with your skin's undertone. When you wear the right metal near your face, your skin looks clearer, your features more defined, and your overall appearance more polished. The wrong metal can make you look yellowish, grayish, or simply "off" in a way that's hard to pinpoint.

---

## Understanding Undertone

Your skin's undertone (the color beneath the surface) determines your metal compatibility:

**Warm undertone:** Skin has golden, peachy, or olive base tones. Veins appear greenish. Gold jewelry looks natural and flattering.

**Cool undertone:** Skin has pink, blue, or reddish base tones. Veins appear blue/purple. Silver and platinum look natural and flattering.

**Neutral undertone:** A mix of warm and cool indicators. Both gold and silver work — lucky you.

---

## Metal Recommendations by Undertone

### Warm Undertones
- **Best:** Yellow gold, brass, copper, bronze, warm rose gold
- **Good:** Champagne gold, antiqued metals, warm-toned titanium
- **Avoid:** Bright silver, cool platinum, white gold (can look harsh)

### Cool Undertones
- **Best:** Silver, platinum, white gold, stainless steel
- **Good:** Cool-toned rose gold, gunmetal, rhodium
- **Avoid:** Yellow gold, brass, copper (can emphasize sallowness)

### Neutral Undertones
- **Best:** Rose gold (the perfect neutral metal), two-tone pieces
- **Good:** Everything — gold, silver, platinum all work
- **Tip:** Match your metal to the occasion rather than sticking to one

## Beyond Metals: Gemstone Compatibility

Gemstones interact with your coloring too. The principle: stones that share your undertone's color family will harmonize with your skin.

**Warm undertones suit:** Citrine, amber, garnet, coral, turquoise, warm-toned sapphire, carnelian, sunstone, warm pearls (cream/champagne)

**Cool undertones suit:** Diamond, sapphire (blue), amethyst, aquamarine, moonstone, ruby (blue-red), emerald (blue-green), cool pearls (white/silver)

**Neutral undertones suit:** Everything, but especially: opal, alexandrite, pearl (any shade), clear diamonds, rose quartz

## Earring Styles by Face Shape

Beyond metal color, earring SHAPE matters for your face:

**Round face:** Long drop earrings, angular/geometric shapes, vertical lines that elongate
**Oval face:** Almost anything works — studs, hoops, drops, chandeliers
**Square face:** Rounded hoops, curved drops, teardrop shapes that soften angles
**Heart face:** Chandelier or bottom-heavy designs that add width at the jaw
**Diamond face:** Studs, short drops, or fan-shaped earrings that don't compete with cheekbones
**Oblong face:** Wide studs, button earrings, short circular hoops that add width

## Necklace Length Guide

Necklace length affects how your neck and face appear:

- **Choker (14-16"):** Best for long necks. Shortens the neck visually.
- **Princess (17-19"):** Universally flattering. The most versatile length.
- **Matinee (20-24"):** Elongates the torso. Great for business-casual.
- **Opera (28-36"):** Creates vertical lines. Flatters shorter torsos.

---

## The Complete Jewelry Formula

Your ideal jewelry combines:
1. **Metal tone** matched to your undertone
2. **Earring shape** matched to your face shape
3. **Gemstone color** from your personal color palette
4. **Scale** matched to your body frame (delicate for petite, statement for larger frames)
