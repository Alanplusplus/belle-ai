## 골드 vs 실버 논쟁

올리브 피부에서는 둘 다 '괜찮지만' 완벽하지 않습니다.

---

## 표준 규칙 실패 이유

**골드:** 노란기 증폭, 누렇게
**실버:** 회색 강조, 칙칙하게

---

## 로즈골드가 이기는 이유

옐로우 골드만큼 노랗지 않고, 실버만큼 회색 강조하지 않음. 그린 캐스트와 조화.

---

## 보석

**Best:** 틸, 가넷, 자수정, 스모키 쿼츠

**주의:** 시트린, 핫핑크, 네온 그린

---

## 핵심

로즈골드가 sweet spot. 보석 색상도 중요.


## gold vs silver for olive

Gold: yellow warmth amplify → sometimes sallow, not bad but not glow
Silver: grey in green-grey cast emphasize → slightly ashen
Result: no-man's land, drawer full of both metals

## rose gold wins

Between gold and copper, pinkish quality:
- no yellow amplification like yellow gold
- no grey emphasis like silver  
- harmonizes with green cast
- works warm/cool/neutral olive

## gemstone guide

**Best:** teal/turquoise, deep burgundy/garnet, amethyst/plum, smoky quartz, muted coral/peach stones
**Caution:** bright citrine, hot pink sapphire, neon green, icy clear + silver

## by subtype & season

Warm olive: rose gold, antique gold, garnet
Cool olive: rose gold, brushed silver, amethyst
Soft Autumn: rose gold, amber | Soft Summer: rose gold, amethyst
Deep Autumn: deep gold, bronze | Deep Winter: silver, deep sapphire
