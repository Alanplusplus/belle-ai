## पुरुषों के लिए रंग विश्लेषण क्यों?

अधिकांश men's style advice fit और occasion पर focus करता है, color के impact को ignore करता है। सही shirt color healthier, authoritative look देता है।

Questions:
- Navy या charcoal suit?
- Silver या gold watch?
- कुछ ties में washed out क्यों?

## Principles (gender-independent)

1. **Undertone** — warm (golden) vs cool (pink/blue)
2. **Depth** — light vs deep
3. **Contrast** — hair, skin, eyes difference
4. **Chroma** — vivid vs muted

## Men's Seasons

### Spring Men
- Golden/peachy skin, warm hair
- **Suits:** tan, camel, warm navy
- **Shirts:** salmon, peach, cream
- **Ties:** coral, orange, gold
- **Metals:** gold watch

### Summer Men
- Pink/rosy cool skin, ash hair
- **Suits:** medium gray, blue-gray
- **Shirts:** sky blue, lavender, soft white
- **Ties:** burgundy, muted blue
- **Metals:** silver, platinum

### Autumn Men
- Golden/olive/bronze skin
- **Suits:** chocolate brown, olive
- **Shirts:** cream, terracotta, olive
- **Ties:** burgundy, forest green, rust
- **Metals:** gold, bronze

### Winter Men
- Cool skin, dark hair
- **Suits:** black, charcoal, navy
- **Shirts:** pure white, icy blue
- **Ties:** true red, royal blue
- **Metals:** silver, steel

## Practical Applications

### Job Interviews
- **Spring:** tan suit + coral tie
- **Summer:** gray suit + burgundy tie
- **Autumn:** brown suit + green tie
- **Winter:** charcoal + red tie

## Common Mistakes

- **"Navy universal"** — warm navy vs cool navy matter
- **"White shirt for everyone"** — pure white is Winter; Spring/Autumn need cream
- **"Black always safe"** — ideal only for Winter men
