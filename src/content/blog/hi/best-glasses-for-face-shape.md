## चश्मा चुनते समय face shape क्यों matter करता है

चश्मा चुनना सिर्फ vision correction या sun protection नहीं — यह हर दिन चेहरे पर पहनने वाला सबसे visible accessory है। सही frames proportions balance, best features highlight कर सकते हैं, और चेहरा slimmer या symmetrical दिखा सकते हैं। गलत? delicate features overwhelm या unwanted proportions emphasize कर सकते हैं।

सिद्धांत straightforward: **frames face shape के साथ contrast करें**, mimic नहीं। Round faces angular frames से benefit — definition add। Angular faces rounded/curved designs से soften। लेकिन details ज़्यादatar guides बताते हैं।

## 6 face shapes और best frames

### Oval Face

Oval सबसे versatile — balanced proportions, slightly narrower forehead और jaw। लगभग कोई भी frame style work, लेकिन कुछ better।

**Best frames:** Walnut-shaped, aviator, cat-eye, rectangular, browline
**क्या देखें:** Face के broadest part जितनी या wider frames। Natural symmetry disturb न करें।
**Avoid:** Oversized frames overwhelm, very narrow frames face wider दिखाएं।

### Round Face

Round faces similar width और length, soft angles। Goal angles add और elongate।

**Best frames:** Rectangular, square, geometric, browline, angular cat-eye
**क्या देखें:** Sharp angles, straight lines। Wider-than-tall। Nose पर high sit कर elongate।
**Avoid:** Round/circular frames (echo shape), small frames face larger, rimless no structure।

### Square Face

Strong jawline, broad forehead, angular features। Curves से soften।

**Best frames:** Round, oval, rimless, aviator, curved cat-eye
**क्या देखें:** Curved/rounded shapes angular jaw contrast। Thin frames no bulk। Slightly wider jaw balance।
**Avoid:** Square/boxy jaw compete, heavy/thick frames, small emphasize width।

### Heart Face (Inverted Triangle)

Wide forehead, prominent cheekbones, narrow chin। Attention downward, top-heaviness balance।

**Best frames:** Bottom-heavy, rimless, aviator, round
**क्या देखें:** Wider at bottom। Light colors, thin rims top। Low-set temples।
**Avoid:** Top-heavy, decorative browlines, cat-eyes forehead width emphasize।

### Diamond Face

Narrow forehead/jaw, wide cheekbones। Softer shapes benefit।

**Best frames:** Oval, cat-eye, rimless, browline
**क्या देखें:** Curved lines cheekbones soften। Brow line detailing। Slightly wider than cheekbones।
**Avoid:** Narrow emphasize cheekbone width, boxy compete angles।

### Oblong (Rectangle) Face

Longer than wide, long nose/chin sometimes। Width add, length break।

**Best frames:** Oversized, round, square with depth, aviator, decorative temples
**क्या देखें:** More depth (height) face shorten visually। Bold colors, thick rims। Decorative side temples width add।
**Avoid:** Small narrow longer face, single-bridge add length।

## Skin tone based frame color

Shape के साथ color matter। Frame color skin undertone complement:

**Warm undertones:** Tortoiseshell, honey, warm brown, gold metal, cream, olive, warm red
**Cool undertones:** Black, gunmetal, silver, cool brown, blue, purple, rose gold
**Neutral undertones:** Lucky — warm और cool दोनों। Clear/crystal frames modern look।
