## Korean Personal Color Analysis क्या है?

Korean personal color analysis (퍼스널 컬러 진단) South Korea में cultural phenomenon बन गया है और K-beauty, K-pop, social media से globally spread हो रहा है।

Western foundations (4/12-season) पर based, distinct characteristics:

- **More granular sub-types** — often 16 or 20 seasons
- **East Asian skin tone focus**
- **K-beauty product integration**
- **Social/lifestyle tool** — dating profiles, ID photos

## Korea obsessed क्यों?

2019–2020 में explode हुआ। Blood type जितना common — personal color जानना।

### Cultural factors:
1. K-beauty culture
2. K-pop idol color analysis — viral content
3. Social media draping videos
4. Dating profiles include color type
5. Professional ID photos by color

**Market:** ₩100+ billion (~$75M USD), hundreds of Seoul salons, ₩200,000–₩500,000/session।

## Korean Color System

| Korean | English | Traits |
|--------|---------|--------|
| 봄 웜톤 | Spring | warm, bright, light |
| 여름 쿨톤 | Summer | cool, soft, light |
| 가을 웜톤 | Autumn | warm, deep, muted |
| 겨울 쿨톤 | Winter | cool, deep, clear |

### 16-Season Extension

Spring: Light, Bright, Warm, Vivid
Summer: Light, Bright, Cool, Mute
Autumn: Mute, Warm, Deep, Strong
Winter: Deep, Cool, Bright, Vivid

## Western से अंतर

- **Asian skin undertones** — warm/cool subtler; yellow ≠ always warm
- **Product integration** — exact shade names (MAC Velvet Teddy, Romand tint)
- **Best Color concept** (베스트 컬러) — single strongest shade for ID photos
