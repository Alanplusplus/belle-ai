## Complete Glow-Up के 8 Dimensions

### 1. Color Season
**Reveals:** 12-season type (e.g. Soft Autumn, Bright Winter) — clothing, makeup, hair, accessories की best palette।
**Impact:** अपने colors wear करने से skin clearer, eyes brighter, overall vibrant। Single most impactful change।

### 2. Face Shape
**Reveals:** Face geometry (oval, round, square, heart, diamond, oblong) और proportions।
**Impact:** Most flattering hairstyles, glasses, earrings, necklines, makeup placement। Shape समझना = हर aesthetic choice bone structure के साथ work।

### 3. Hair Color & Style Direction
**Reveals:** Skin undertone complement hair colors, face shape flattering styles।
**Impact:** Hair सबसे बड़ा daily "accessory"। Right color/style years off, face brighten, aesthetic define।

### 4. Makeup Guide
**Reveals:** Best lip colors, eyeshadow palettes, blush tones, application techniques specific features के लिए।
**Impact:** Personalized makeup enhance, mask नहीं। Best lip shade alone countless failed purchases save।

### 5. Glasses & Eyewear
**Reveals:** Face shape और coloring complement frame shapes/colors।
**Impact:** Glasses wearers के लिए most visible style choice। Right frames signature accessory, necessity से beyond।

### 6. Jewelry & Metals
**Reveals:** Metal tones (gold/silver/rose gold), gemstones, earring styles coloring/face shape complement।
**Impact:** Small accessories huge impact। Right metal skin glow; wrong drained।

### 7. Wardrobe Colors
**Reveals:** Curated capsule wardrobe palette — best colors category-wise (basics, accents, statements)।
**Impact:** Cohesive wardrobe, everything mix & match, decision fatigue reduce, always put-together।

### 8. Style Personality
**Reveals:** Aesthetic archetype — silhouettes, textures, vibe naturally features/energy align।
**Impact:** Others पर great, you पर flat things buy stop। Shopping clear direction।
