## Ek Chehra, Kayi Occasions

Wahi red lip jo cocktail party par heads turn karti hai, job interview mein aggressively out of place lag sakti hai. Brunch wala fresh «no-makeup makeup» black-tie gala mein underdone dikha sakta hai. Context matter karta hai — aur occasion ke hisaab se makeup adapt karna ek skill hai.

Lekin challenge yeh hai: zyada tar guides generic advice deti hain («din mein natural, shaam ko bold») bina aapke individual features, skin tone ya undertone ke. Ek chehre par «natural look» ke liye bilkul alag products aur techniques chahiye.

---

## Occasion Spectrum

Occasions ko minimal se maximal spectrum par socho:

```
Minimal ←→ Maximal

Office → Brunch → Date → Wedding Guest → Evening Gala → Festival
```

Har step par zyada color, shimmer, drama — lekin hamesha filter karke jo AAPKE chehre par actually flatters.

---

## Occasion-by-Occasion

### Office / Professional
**Goal:** Polished, put-together, distracting nahi
**Focus:** Even skin, groomed brows, subtle definition (mascara, nude lip, light contour)
**Skip:** Shimmer, bold lip, heavy eye makeup

### Brunch / Casual
**Goal:** Fresh, dewy, effortless
**Focus:** Glowy skin, cream blush, tinted lip, fluffy brows

### Date Night
**Goal:** Sabse attractive version — soft, alluring
**Focus:** Eyes YA lips (dono nahi). Smoky eye ya bold lip, luminous skin

### Job Interview
**Goal:** Confidence, competence, approachable
**Focus:** Flawless base, natural brow, MLBB lip

### Wedding Guest
**Goal:** Elegant, photographable, long-lasting
**Focus:** Buildable coverage, setting spray, waterproof mascara

### Evening Gala / Black Tie
**Goal:** Glamour, impact, red-carpet-ready
**Focus:** Dramatic eyes YA bold lip, sculpted contour

### Festival / Party
**Goal:** Creative, expressive, fun
**Focus:** Glitter, bold color, waterproof sab kuch

---

## Skin Tone Factor

**Warm undertones:** «Neutral» warm nude hai. «Bold» tomato/brick red.
**Cool undertones:** «Neutral» rose-toned. «Bold» berry, wine, blue-red.
**Neutral undertones:** Dono directions mein flexibility.
