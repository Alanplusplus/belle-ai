## olive가 season typing 어려운 이유

Undertone: green masks warm/cool | Value: all depths | Chroma: inherent muted → rarely Spring/Bright

## 4 common seasons detail

**Soft Autumn:** muted warm medium, olive green/warm taupe/terracotta, most common
**Soft Summer:** muted cool medium, dusty rose/soft teal/mauve
**Deep Autumn:** muted warm deep, dark chocolate/burnt sienna
**Deep Winter:** muted-cool deep high contrast, deep burgundy/dark emerald

## rare seasons

Spring (clarity/saturation), True (clear undertone), Light (fair olive → Soft not Light)

## narrow down 5 steps

1. confirm olive 2. warm vs cool olive 3. depth/contrast 4. drape test 5. AI analysis

## styling by season

Soft Autumn: earthy neutrals, terracotta blush, brick lips, bronze eyes
Soft Summer: mushroom grey, soft teal, dusty rose, taupe eyes
Deep Autumn: dark chocolate, deep berry, copper eyes
Deep Winter: soft black, deep burgundy, smoky deep eyes
