## Blonde shade choose करना इतना hard क्यों है

Blonde एक color नहीं — icy platinum से deep honey और strawberry gold तक spectrum है — हर shade warm-to-cool axis पर अलग point पर।

Brown या black में depth decide करते हैं। Blonde में depth *और* temperature simultaneously। Level-9 ash blonde और level-9 golden blonde same lightness, completely different colors।

Blonde almost always bleaching require करता है, underlying warmth — yellow, orange, gold — reveal। Many "bad blonde" experiences target shade नहीं, journey — insufficient lift, skipped toning।

Blonde darker colors जितना forgiving नहीं। Slightly too-cool = "dull." Slightly too-warm = "brassy" या "cheap."

## हर color season के best blonde shades

### Spring (warm, light, clear)

**Light Spring** — Champagne, light golden, strawberry

**True Spring** — Honey, bright golden, butter

**Bright Spring** — Bright golden, copper-blonde

### Summer (cool, light, muted)

**Light Summer** — Light ash, soft beige, pearl

**True Summer** — Ash, cool beige

**Soft Summer** — Soft ash, dusty beige

### Autumn (warm, deep, muted)

**Soft Autumn** — Dark honey, caramel, bronde

**True Autumn** — Golden bronde, deep honey

**Dark Autumn** — Dark caramel, toffee bronde

### Winter (cool, deep, clear)

**Dark Winter** — Cool beige, ash bronde

**Bright Winter** — Bright platinum, cool champagne
